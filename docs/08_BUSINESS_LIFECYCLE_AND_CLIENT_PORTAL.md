# Business Lifecycle and Client Portal

Lifecycle: lead → qualification → discovery → proposal/negotiation → signed contract → onboarding/access/assets → scope freeze → milestones/tasks → staging preview → feedback/revisions/bug reports → UAT acceptance → optional production hosting → invoice/payment reconciliation → handover → warranty → maintenance/support/renewal → closure/archive/retention.

Client portal features:
- project overview and milestone status;
- requirements and scope baseline;
- secure asset intake and access checklist;
- staging preview link with expiry/access policy;
- feedback by page/module with attachments;
- bug reports with severity/reproduction steps;
- UAT checklist and acceptance;
- deliverables and versioned handover;
- invoices/payment status;
- hosting/maintenance plan and incidents where subscribed.

Client-facing communications are drafts until approved under the relevant gate. Do not expose internal prompts, secrets, logs or other client data.
