# Data Retention, Export and Deletion

Define retention by data class and contract. Provide export for client-owned records and deliverables. Deletion workflow must include primary DB, object storage, derived indexes, backups according to expiry schedule and provider copies where contract/terms permit. Preserve legally required accounting/audit records only under reviewed retention policy. Record deletion request, approval, scope, execution evidence and exceptions. Do not claim deletion from immutable/offsite backups until retention expiry or verified purge.
