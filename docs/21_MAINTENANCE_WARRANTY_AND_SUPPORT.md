# Warranty, Maintenance and Support

Separate commercial scopes:
- warranty: defects against signed acceptance criteria within stated period;
- maintenance: patching, dependency review, routine checks and planned updates;
- hosting: compute/storage/network and deployment operations;
- monitoring: alerts and incident triage;
- support: user assistance and technical questions;
- enhancements: new scope, quoted separately.

Define coverage hours, response targets, severity, exclusions, client response obligations, backup ownership, third-party outages, change windows, emergency rates and renewal/cancellation. Do not promise 24x7 human response unless staffed and contracted. AI monitoring may be continuous, but human response coverage must be explicit.
