# Digital Marketing Execution Workflows

Service families: technical/on-page/local/e-commerce/programmatic SEO and GEO; copy/content/visual/video/audio; organic social/community; paid ads; WhatsApp/SMS/Telegram; email lifecycle; CRO; analytics/attribution; ORM/PR/affiliate.

Each campaign requires brief, target, audience/consent basis, client account authorization, deliverables, review gates, measurement plan and reporting. Paid ads remain PAUSED until client budget and founder launch approval. Use official platform APIs or approved tools. No unofficial bulk messaging, artificial warm-up, unsolicited outreach, fake reviews, ranking guarantees or anti-bot bypass.
